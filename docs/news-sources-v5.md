# News sources v5 (phase 7): research and proposal

Status: **proposal for Ondřej's review, not enabled.** Config proposal: `seeds/feeds_v5_proposed.yaml` (not
loaded by the code; `seeds/feeds.yaml` and `rss.py` are unchanged).

Method (2026-10-03):
- Every feed was fetched with the project User-Agent
  `klugscheisser-athlete/0.1 (+https://github.com/andres-cerny/klugscheisser_athlete)` and parsed with
  feedparser. "Verified" means HTTP 200, valid RSS/Atom, and items with links and dates. The date is the newest
  item.
- robots.txt was read for the feed host and for the article host. Each was checked for `*`, our UA and AI agents
  (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-User, Google-Extended, CCBot), with wildcard rules evaluated.
- Terms were read on the publisher's own pages, with at most a few pages per site.
- `fetch_article: true` is proposed only where robots.txt allows article pages for our UA **and** the terms (or
  a published legal notice) explicitly allow automated reading. If the terms are silent, the feed stays teaser
  only.
- `source_kind`: `public` = public broadcaster, `media` = newspaper, `boulevard` = tabloid, `official` =
  federation/club. `srf` stays for SRF only.

Before building:
1. **Language filter.** `rss.py` keeps only items that match German women's terms (`WOMEN` regex). Feeds in fr,
   it, sv, fi, cs, da, no, sl, hr, sk, pl, nl and en need per-language women's and hockey/ski terms, or the
   athlete-name match only. Otherwise they yield nothing.
2. **General feeds.** Several feeds are all-sport or all-news (Berner Zeitung, STVR, NOS, ZDF, ORF, DR, RAI,
   TVP, RTBF, Sporza). They rely on that text filter.
3. **STVR** has sport only as `<category>Šport</category>`. A category filter would need a code change.
4. **Duplicates.** Luzerner Zeitung and Zuger Zeitung serve identical items (same `ld.<id>`). Keep one or
   dedupe by id. 24 heures and Tribune de Genève share most hockey items under different hosts.
5. **BBC** forbids using even RSS metadata for business use without permission. Do not enable without asking.
   VRT's terms forbid automated "raadplegen" (consulting), which may cover feed polling as well.
6. **Permission requests** would unlock article text for the most relevant outlets:
   - SRG SSR: partnermanagement@srgssr.ch, which covers RTS and RSI.
   - CH Media.
   - TX Group, which covers Berner Zeitung, 24 heures, TdG and Le Matin.

## Notes per finding

- **CBC blocks our User-Agent.** Both the feeds and robots.txt are refused (server disconnect) for the project
  UA, while a browser UA gets 200. This is the only source with UA blocking.
- **Silent terms are not consent.** ČT sport, STVR and USA Hockey have open robots.txt but no clause allowing
  automated reading, so they stay teaser only. ČT is the most useful of the three to ask.
- **SVT and Sportschau are the only explicit allowances.** Both rest on statements in robots.txt (SVT: "real-time
  retrieval"; ARD: "RAG or grounding … attributed to its original source"). Neither allows training or
  storage beyond what we quote. If Ondřej prefers the stricter reading, set both to false.
- **Off-season.** The France Télévisions ski alpin feed is from 2026-08-05. The feed is valid; its items
  are older because it is off-season.
