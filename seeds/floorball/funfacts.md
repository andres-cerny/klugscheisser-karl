# Unihockey / Floorball: verified history and fun facts (Men's WFC 2026)

Compiled 2026-11-24 for Klugscheisser Karl. Every item names its source and the source's date.
Items marked **[unverified]** come from a single secondary source (Wikipedia or a blog) and should be
checked before air. Opinions from blogs are labelled as such.

## The tournament

1. **16th men's WFC**, Tampere, 5-13 Dec 2026, 16 teams. The organisers call it the biggest sporting event in Finland in 2026.
   Source: IFF, "Here are the groups for the Men's WFC 2026", 2026-03-05, https://floorball.sport/here-are-the-groups-for-the-mens-wfc-2026/
2. **Opening game Finland v Switzerland** (Sat 5 Dec, 19:00 local / 18:00 Swiss time, Hakametsä) is sold out. Extra standing tickets went on sale on 23 Nov. The evening also marks 40 years of Finnish floorball and premieres the official theme song.
   Source: official event site freeoflimits.fi, 2026-11-23, https://freeoflimits.fi/kovan-kysynnan-vuoksi-loppuunmyytyyn-suomi-sveitsi-otteluun-avataan-seisomakatsomo/
3. **Hakametsä is Finland's first ice hall.** Group games are played there and at Kauppi Sports Center. Everything from the quarterfinals on is played at Nokia Arena.
   Source: freeoflimits.fi venues page, retrieved 2026-11-24, https://freeoflimits.fi/faneille/tapahtumapaikat/
4. **Mascot "Vekku"**, a fox designed by Mauri Kunnas, one of Finland's best-loved children's authors (Koiramäki books). A reader from Kokkola picked the name from almost 1,000 suggestions. Vekku will also appear at the Women's WFC 2027 in Turku and then become the mascot of all Finnish national teams.
   Source: freeoflimits.fi, Nov 2026, https://freeoflimits.fi/mauri-kunnaksen-suunnittelema-mm-kisamaskotti-on-vekku/
5. **Finland v Norway on 6 December is played on Finnish Independence Day**, with a family event day at Hakametsä.
   Source: freeoflimits.fi, 2026-11-20, https://freeoflimits.fi/siniverkkarimiehen-money-bowling-itsenaisyyspaivana-hakametsassa/
6. **A Swiss referee pair is at the WFC:** Josef Fässler / Benjamin Schläpfer. It is their second men's WFC (first in 2024). They refereed the last three Swiss Superfinals and the final of the U19 men's WFC 2025.
   Source: swiss unihockey via unihockey.ch, 2026-09-09, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/zweite-a-wm-fr-fsslerschlpfer-28535/
7. **Canada took the last spot**, winning all three AMER qualification games against USA (13-1, 8-2 and a third win) in Georgetown, Texas, in March 2026.
   Source: IFF, 2026-03-21, https://floorball.sport/canada-secures-final-spot-in-wfc-2026/
8. **Qualification oddities (Feb 2026):**
   - Sweden won its group in Trenčín with 100:1 goals, including 44-0 against Liechtenstein and 46-0 against Iceland. They played with borrowed sticks and warm-up shirts with taped-on numbers because their luggage was lost.
   - Liechtenstein turned 0-2 into 3-2 against Iceland in the last seven minutes, a rare win.
   - Norway, a top-8 team, got through only as "lucky loser".

   Source: unihockey.ch, 2026-02-10, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/15-von-16-wm-pltzen-vergeben-28124/
9. **Record entry:** 35 teams registered for WFC 2026 qualification. Solomon Islands and Hong Kong, China took part for the first time.
   Source: IFF "Event Evolution", retrieved 2026-11-24, https://floorball.sport/world-floorball-championships/wfc-men/
10. **Last WFC in the two-year rhythm before the reform.** From 2029 the IFF moves to a "7-event structure". WFCs will be held only every four years, with 12-team European Championships in between that also serve as WFC qualification, plus a new "World Trophy" for developing nations. The men's WFC 2028 goes to Czechia. That is Czechia's 4th men's WFC after 1998, 2008 and 2018; Prague or the new 13,000+ arena in Brno are the candidate venues.
    Sources: unihockey.ch, 2026-02-02, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/neue-iff-eventstruktur-ab-2029-28107/ ; unihockey.ch, 2025-09, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/mnner-wm-2028-in-prag-27714/
11. **Do NOT use:** "Unihockey-WM in den USA" (WFC 2031/32 in the USA, with Trump quotes) is an April Fools' joke published on 2026-04-01.
    https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/unihockey-wm-in-den-usa-28280/

## WFC history and records

12. **Only two champions in 15 editions:** Sweden 10 titles, Finland 5. Medal table: Sweden 10-5-0, Finland 5-7-3, Czechia 0-2-4, Switzerland 0-1-7, Norway 0-0-1.
    Sources: IFF WFC Men page, https://floorball.sport/world-floorball-championships/wfc-men/ ; unihockey.ch medal table, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/medaillen/ (both retrieved 2026-11-24)
13. **Sweden won the first six WFCs (1996-2006).** Finland's first title came in 2008 in Prague/Ostrava. That WFC was also the first with 100,000 spectators.
    Source: unihockey.ch, 2025-09, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/mnner-wm-2028-in-prag-27714/
14. **Spectator record: 181,571 at WFC 2018 in Prague**, with 16,276 at the final in the O2 Arena.
    Source: unihockey.ch, 2025-09 (same article).
15. **Finland is the defending champion.** It beat Sweden 5-4 in overtime in the 2024 Malmö final.
    Source: Wikipedia (en) "Men's World Floorball Championship", retrieved 2026-11-24; champion status confirmed by IFF 2026-03-05.
16. **All-time WFC points leader is Swiss: Matthias Hofbauer**, 97 points (48 goals + 49 assists) in 57 games, 2000-2018. Hofbauer is now swiss unihockey's head of men's national teams ("Leiter Nationalteams Männer").
    Sources: IFF Men's WFC Records, https://floorball.sport/wfc-records/mens-wfc-records/ (retrieved 2026-11-24) ; unihockey.ch, 2026-09-11, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/schnbeck-hrt-nach-wm-2026-auf-28542/
17. **Emanuel Antener (SUI) holds the single-tournament records** for points (26 = 9+17) and assists (17), both at the home WFC 2012. He also shares the career assists record (50 in only 30 games, 2008-16) with Finland's Mika Kohonen (50 in 60 games).
    Source: IFF Men's WFC Records (as above). The IFF counts A-division games 1996-2008 and final-round games 2010-2024.
18. **Most goals:** Kim Nilsson (SWE, 58 goals in 42 games, 2010-22) and Willy Fauskanger (NOR, 58 in 54 games, 1996-2014). **Most goals in one WFC:** Mathias Glass (NOR), 16 in 2022.
    Source: IFF Men's WFC Records.
19. **Iron man:** Ketil Kronberg (NOR) played 12 WFCs (2000-2022) and a record 64 WFC games.
    Source: IFF Men's WFC Records.
20. **The IFF all-time top 10 includes two active stars of WFC 2026 opponents** (check their rosters): Michal Dudovic (SVK, 69 points in 25 games) and Patrik Kareliusson (EST, 69 points in 26 games). Dudovic scored against Switzerland in the 3-4 loss in Biglen on 16 Oct 2026.
    Sources: IFF Men's WFC Records ; unihockey.ch, 2026-10-16, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/schweiz-verliert-gegen-aufsssige-slowaken-28645/

## Switzerland at WFCs

21. **Switzerland's best WFC ever: silver in 1998 in Prague** (final lost 3-10 to Sweden). They reached the final by beating Finland in a semifinal penalty shootout. It is still the only time Switzerland has reached a WFC final.
    **Correction to the brief:** 1998 in Prague was silver, not bronze. Switzerland's "Prague bronze" came in **2018** (bronze game 4-2 against hosts Czechia, after losing the semifinal to Sweden on penalties).
    Sources: IFF/unihockey.ch medal tables ; unihockey.ch blog "Prager Geschichten" (Damian Keller, 2018) https://unihockey.ch/blogs/prager-geschichten-21230/ ; unihockey.ch 2025-09 (27714)
22. **8 WFC medals: 1 silver (1998) and 7 bronze (2000, 2002, 2006, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2018).** Switzerland placed in every edition: 5, 2, 3, 3, 4, 3, 3, 4, 3, 4, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5 (1996-2024).
    Sources: IFF ; unihockey.ch medals ; Wikipedia participation table.
23. **No medal since 2018.** The 2020 WFC (played 2021) and 2022 both ended in 4th place. Malmö 2024 ended in 5th, the worst Swiss placing since the first WFC in 1996. That was the main reason Switzerland dropped from pot 1 to pot 2 for the 2026 draw (Latvia took its place).
    Sources: unihockey.ch, 2026-03-05, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/gruppenauslosung-wm-2026-28199/ ; swiss unihockey team page https://www.swissunihockey.ch/de/nationalteams/maenner/
24. **Home WFCs:** 2004 (Zürich/Kloten): 4th, lost the bronze game 7-8 on penalties to Finland. 2012 (Zürich/Bern): bronze, 8-0 against Germany. 2022 (Zürich/Winterthur): 4th.
    Source: Wikipedia (en) results table, retrieved 2026-11-24 **[bronze-game scores from Wikipedia only]**
25. **The long wait against Sweden:** the first Switzerland-Sweden international was on 14 Feb 1987 in Zürich (4-8). Switzerland's first-ever win came only in the 67th meeting, **7-5 at the Euro Floorball Tour in Kirchberg on 5 Nov 2017**, with Federal Councillor and sports minister Guy Parmelin in the hall. Paolo Riedi scored 1-0 after 36 seconds, and Kim Nilsson scored his 100th international goal in the same game. It is still Switzerland's only win against Sweden (as of Nov 2025).
    Sources: unihockey.ch blog "Historischer Sieg" (Damian Keller, Nov 2017) https://unihockey.ch/blogs/historischer-sieg-19640/ ; IFF archive "November 2017 internationals weekend results" (2017-11-06, https://archive.floorball.sport/2017/11/06/november-2017-internationals-weekend-results/, the archive domain no longer resolves; details via search snippet) ; unihockey.ch 2025-11 ("einziger Sieg ... am 5. November 2017 in Kirchberg") https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/mnner-nationalteams-im-einsatz-27907/
26. **Sweden at WFCs against Switzerland**, per Keller 2017: 1998 final 10-3; semifinals 2000 8-2, 2014 10-1, 2016 7-2; close semifinals 2008 3-2 OT and 2010 3-2. Sweden also won the World Games 2017 final 7-5. **[blog source; cross-check with wfc_history_detail.yaml]**
27. **Swiss women are world champions (2005 and 2025).** In the 2025 WFC semifinal against Czechia they came back from 1-6 to win in overtime. Commentator's line: "Er ist drin! Drin! Drin!" The men have never won gold.
    Sources: unihockey.ch blog "Das Wunder von Neuenburg" (Keller) https://unihockey.ch/blogs/das-wunder-von-neuenburg-22566/ ; unihockey.ch, 2026-09-07, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/schwedische-teams-dominieren-eft-28532/ (mentions "zwei Schweizer Weltmeistertitel" of the women) ; SRF headline "2. WM-Titel nach 2005" (srf.ch)

## The 2026 Swiss team

28. **Youngest Swiss WFC squad in eight championships**, with an average age of "knapp 25" (swiss unihockey). 10 players will play their first A-WFC. By our own count from the birth dates, the average is 25.4 at the start of the tournament. The youngest is Mika Hänni (born 4 Nov 2007, aged 19). The oldest is Marco Louis (born 6 Apr 1994, aged 32).
    Source: swiss unihockey via unihockey.ch, 2026-11-05, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/mit-zehn-debtanten-nach-tampere-28693/ ; birth dates from unihockey.ch club rosters.
29. **Two pairs of brothers: Noël and Elia Seiler, Janis and Niklas Graf.** They first played together in an A international on 5 Sep 2026 against Czechia (2-1 win at the EFT in Chomutov). Noël Seiler won his 50th cap in the same game.
    Source: unihockey.ch, 2026-09-05, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/schweiz-gewinnt-dank-unterzahl-tor-gegen-tschechien-28527/
30. **Only three players remain from the home WFC 2022:** Jan Zaugg, Noël Seiler and Jan Bürki. Many experienced players retired after 2022.
    Source: unihockey.ch, 2026-03-05 (28199) ; unihockey.ch 2026-09-11 (28542)
31. **Six Swiss players play in Sweden:** Jan Bürki (FBC Kalmarsund), David Hermle (Växjö Vipers), Gianluca Persici (Mullsjö AIS), Pascal Schmuki (Storvreta IBK), Noël Seiler (IBF Falun), Matteo Steiner (Nykvarns IBF).
    Source: WFC squad announcement, 2026-11-05 (28693).
32. **Swedish arenas, Swiss gyms.** At WFC 2024, then-captain Noël Seiler (with Thorengruppen, Umeå) said almost every SSL team has its own stadium and that Thorengruppen has "das beste Stadion der Welt". In Switzerland some top-league games are still played in school gyms.
    Source: Blick, 2024-12-07, https://www.blick.ch/sport/unihockey/zur-wm-in-malmoe-drei-nati-cracks-leben-im-norden-den-unihockey-traum/nbdmwzh
33. **Pascal Schmuki won the Champions Cup 2026 with Storvreta.** He was U19 WFC silver medallist and All-Star in 2023. **[unverified: German Wikipedia only]** https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_Schmuki
34. **The coach says goodbye:** Swede Johan Schönbeck (in charge since 2023) leaves after the WFC. He wants to spend more time with his family in Växjö. His first WFC (2024) came with 12 debutants and ended in 5th place.
    Source: swiss unihockey via unihockey.ch, 2026-09-11, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/schnbeck-hrt-nach-wm-2026-auf-28542/
35. **Former Swiss coaches at WFCs:** Petteri Nykky (FIN) in 2014; David Jansson (SWE) in 2016, 2018, 2020/21 and 2022. Switzerland has had Nordic head coaches since at least 2014.
    Source: unihockey.ch WFC event pages, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/events/
36. **Form before Tampere (2026):**
    - WFC qualification in Liepāja: 27-1 v Netherlands, 21-0 v Great Britain, 6-2 v Norway, 3-4 PSO v Latvia. Niklas Graf scored after 4 seconds against GB.
    - EFT in Chomutov: **4-3 v world champion Finland** (winner by Noah Püntener), 2-1 v Czechia (short-handed winner by Noah Siegenthaler), 2-8 v Sweden.
    - Biglen: 3-4 v Slovakia, 4-3 PSO v Czechia. These were the first internationals ever in Biglen, and the new WFC shirts were worn for the first time.

    Sources: unihockey.ch 28116, 28117, 28118, 28120, 28523, 28527, 28529, 28645, 28647, 28415.
37. **Head-to-head against Latvia, the key group opponent:** under Schönbeck, three straight losses (up to Malmö 2024), then three straight wins, then the 3-4 PSO loss in Feb 2026. The Group A game against Latvia (6 Dec, 14:00 Swiss time) probably decides 2nd place and a direct QF spot.
    Sources: unihockey.ch, 2026-03-05 (28199) and 2026-04-10, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/wm-spielplan-der-mnner-bekannt-28300/
38. **Switzerland v Norway, last WFC:** 4-4 in the Malmö 2024 group stage, then 6-0 in the placement game.
    Source: unihockey.ch, 2026-02-04, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/wm-quali-in-lettland-auch-u17-und-u19-im-einsatz-28114/
39. **Nationality switch:** Linus Hedlund (Zug United) won U19 WFC bronze with Switzerland against Sweden in May 2025. He was then called up by Sweden, his father's country, for the EFT in Nov 2025.
    Source: unihockey.ch, 2025-11, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/mnner-nationalteams-im-einsatz-27907/

## World Games (context)

40. **World Games 2025 Chengdu (men):** Sweden won gold. Switzerland was 4th: 0-4 against Finland in the semifinal, then 2-7 against Czechia in the bronze game, after trailing 0-5 shortly after the first break. Floorball is confirmed for the World Games 2029 in Karlsruhe (19-29 Jul 2029), its 4th straight edition. Karlsruhe is the first city to host the World Games twice.
    Sources: unihockey.ch 2025-08 (27674, 27676) ; unihockey.ch 2026-09-08, https://unihockey.ch/nationalteams/mnner-a/news/unihockey-definitiv-an-den-world-games-2029-28534/
